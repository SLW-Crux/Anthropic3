# Stage 2B — Segment economics, profit pools and bottlenecks

**Required context:** `00_MASTER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`; the approved Stage 2A handoff, value-chain map and demand scenarios; the Stage 1 ledgers. Start with the capability declaration.

## Mandate
Determine where sustainable economic value is captured across the value chain mapped in Stage 2A. Judge segments on their economics only. **Do not assess PE dealability or rank PE theses** — that is Stage 3.

Analyse in depth the segments the Stage 2A handoff prioritised; cover the others in one line each with the reason they were deprioritised.

## Research tasks
1. **Segment financial structure.** For each prioritised segment: concentration, customer bargaining power, barriers to entry, switching costs and qualification lock-in, IP, gross and operating margins, recurring or aftermarket revenue, utilisation and yield sensitivity, capex intensity, R&D burden, working capital, cash conversion and return on invested capital.
2. **Evidence for those economics.** Use reported financials of real companies wherever possible — listed pure-plays, segment disclosures, filed accounts of private companies where registries publish them. Where you must use a diversified company or an adjacent industry as a proxy, label it as a proxy and say why it may mislead.
3. **Temporary shortage versus durable bottleneck.** For each apparent bottleneck: who is adding capacity, how much and by when (announced versus installed), how long qualification takes, and the scenario in which today's pricing power disappears. Include vertical integration by large customers or suppliers.
4. **Who holds the profit.** Estimate, as ranges, how margin is distributed along the chain today and how each Stage 2A scenario would shift it. Avoid double counting.
5. **Cycle and downside behaviour.** How each segment's revenue and margin behaved in past downturns (for example the 2001 optical bust, 2019 and 2022–23 inventory corrections), where data exists.
6. **Regional economics.** Where evidence allows, note how the economics of Asia Pacific ex-China suppliers differ from global peers (scale, customer access, labour and capital cost, subsidy), and how exposed each segment is to price competition from Chinese suppliers.
7. **Manufacturing-partner economics.** For contract, licensed and joint-venture manufacturing of Western-designed photonics products: typical margins, customer concentration, contract terms, who owns IP and tooling, who funds capex, switching and re-qualification costs, and returns on capital. Use real companies (for example listed Asian optical contract manufacturers and assembly/test houses) and say how they differ by segment. Identify what distinguishes the few high-return manufacturing partners from commodity ones.
8. **Disconfirming evidence** for each segment judged attractive, and the best case for each judged unattractive.

## Required deliverables
A. **Segment economics matrix (file where possible):** one row per segment; growth, margin, cash conversion, capex, concentration, barriers, substitution risk, cyclicality. Every figure tagged as a verified company number (with source ID), a proxy, or an assumption. Confidence per row.

B. **Profit-pool and bottleneck map:** where value accrues, durability, capacity coming, evidence, counter-argument.

C. **Structurally attractive and unattractive segments**, on economics alone, each with confidence and the main reason it could be wrong.

D. **Data-poor segments:** segments that may be attractive but lack public financial evidence, and what would be needed to judge them.

E. **Gate self-assessment and Stage Handoff** with the questions Stage 3 must resolve.

## Gate pass criteria
- Each prioritised segment has at least one real company financial data point, or is listed as data-poor.
- Every figure in the matrix is tagged verified / proxy / assumption.
- Each bottleneck has a stated collapse scenario and a view on timing.
- Each "attractive" judgement is paired with a disconfirming argument.
- No PE dealability assessment, thesis ranking or company targeting has been done.

**Stop after Stage 2B.**
