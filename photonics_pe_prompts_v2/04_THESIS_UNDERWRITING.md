# Stage 4 — Investment thesis deep dives and financial underwriting

**Required context:** `00_MASTER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`; the approved Stage 3 shortlist, handoff and scorecard; Stage 1–2B ledgers and files. Do not silently replace or redefine the approved theses. Start with the capability declaration.

## Mandate
Determine whether each shortlisted thesis can generate compelling **risk-adjusted equity returns** from achievable operations, rather than from optimistic technology adoption or exit-multiple expansion.

These models describe an **archetype business**, not a real company. Their purpose is to show which assumptions the returns depend on, not to predict a return.

## Research tasks (same structure for every shortlisted thesis)
1. **Archetype definition.** Products, customers, qualification cycle, commercial maturity, competitive advantage, ownership and control route, home country or countries within Asia Pacific ex-China, and deal-size band (use the Fund Mandate if provided, otherwise the bands in the master instructions).
2. **Operating economics.** Revenue drivers, pricing, margins, customer and supplier concentration, cyclicality, utilisation, yield, capex (maintenance versus growth), working capital, R&D and cash conversion — anchored to the Stage 2B matrix.
3. **Comparables and precedents.** Listed comparables and **completed** transactions only, each with a source retrieved in this session and its date. Record trading multiples with the retrieval date. Comparables and precedents may be global, but show regional ones separately and explain how valuation, governance and sale practices in the relevant Asia Pacific markets differ (for example cross-shareholdings, founder or conglomerate control, take-private rules). Explain the limits of comparing pure-plays with diversified groups. Where no data exists, say so; do not estimate a multiple from memory.
4. **Illustrative model** over 5 and 7 years: volume, price, mix, EBITDA, capex, working capital, tax, cash flow and funding need. Deliver as XLSX or CSV with formulas visible, or as calculation tables showing each step. Every input is tagged **sourced** (with ID) or **assumption**.
5. **Capital structure.** Use buyout debt only if cash flows support it; otherwise model equity-heavy or growth structures. Interest rate, leverage, amortisation and covenants are assumptions; state the date and basis of any market reference used.
6. **Returns.** Base, upside and downside cases as gross MOIC and IRR, decomposed into earnings growth, cash generation/deleveraging and multiple change. Show a base case with **no multiple expansion**.
7. **Break-even analysis.** For each thesis state the conditions under which returns clear each reference hurdle (or the Fund Mandate hurdle if provided): maximum entry multiple, minimum margin, minimum growth, maximum capex. This matters more than the point estimates.
8. **Stress tests.** Delayed adoption, price erosion, overcapacity, yield shortfall, capex overrun, loss of the largest customer, technology substitution, a two-year pause in AI capex, aggressive price competition from Chinese suppliers, currency movement against the fund's reporting currency, and a weak exit market.
9. **Value-creation levers** with owner, KPI, cost, timing and expected EBITDA or cash effect. Separate what management and the sponsor control from what the market must deliver.
10. **Exit.** Buyer categories supported by completed historical deals, not strategic logic alone. Assume Chinese buyers are unavailable at exit unless the user says otherwise, and say how much that narrows the buyer universe.
11. **If a manufacturing-partner thesis is shortlisted,** additionally model: customer concentration and loss of the largest Western partner; contract renewal and price-down clauses; who pays for capex and tooling; ramp and qualification delays; and the value of converting from pure contract work to licensed or co-owned products. State what contractual protections would be needed for the returns to hold.
12. **Go/no-go tests**, diligence priorities and the information management would have to provide before a credible IC decision.

## Required deliverables
A. **Thesis papers** in identical structure: evidence, economics, dealability, risks, exit.

B. **Models (files where possible)** with sourced inputs versus assumptions and a returns decomposition.

C. **Break-even and sensitivity comparison** identifying the two or three assumptions that dominate each thesis.

D. **Investment conditions, kill criteria (`K-…`) and diligence requests** per thesis.

E. **Relative underwriting attractiveness**, including where the evidence does not support a conclusion.

F. **Gate self-assessment and Stage Handoff** stating the archetypes and the valuation and ownership questions Stage 5 must test.

## Gate pass criteria
- Every model input is tagged sourced or assumption.
- Every comparable and precedent has a source retrieved in this session, or the absence of data is stated.
- A no-multiple-expansion case and break-even conditions are shown for each thesis.
- The AI-capex-pause stress test is run for each thesis.
- Returns are presented as conditional on stated assumptions, not as forecasts.
- No real company is named as available for acquisition.

**Stop after Stage 4.**
