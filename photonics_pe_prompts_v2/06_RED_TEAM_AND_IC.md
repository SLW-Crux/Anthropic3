# Stage 6 — Independent challenge and final investment committee thesis

This stage has two parts with a gate between them: **6A red team**, then **6B IC package**.

---

# Stage 6A — Independent red team

**Run this in a fresh session that has not seen the earlier working.** Provide only: `00_MASTER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, this prompt, the Stage 1–5 handoffs, and the evidence, claims, assumption, kill-criteria and company files. Do not provide earlier chat transcripts. Start with the capability declaration.

**Recommended model:** Fable 5.2.

## Role
You are the skeptical investment committee member. You did not write this work and have no stake in it. Your job is to find where it is wrong, weak or overconfident. Agreeing with the prior stages is acceptable only where you have tested a point and it held. Do not assume earlier recommendations are correct.

## Tasks
1. **Re-verify a sample.** Pick the ten claims the surviving theses depend on most and check each against its source in this session. Report any that do not hold up as stated.
2. **Demand.** Challenge market size, adoption timing, dependence on AI capex, the demand scenarios and any remaining double counting.
3. **Bottlenecks.** Which are temporary? Does announced capacity or vertical integration remove the expected pricing power?
4. **Technology.** Copper and other competing approaches; delays to CPO, NPO and optical I/O; yield, scale, qualification and reliability constraints.
5. **Company economics.** Recurring capex and R&D, customer concentration, financeability, management dependence.
6. **Underwriting.** Comparable selection, entry price, leverage, synergies, model assumptions and reliance on exit multiples. Recalculate at least one headline return figure from the model inputs.
7. **Deal reality.** Does every proposed target genuinely satisfy the geography rule — Asia Pacific outside China, with no Chinese shareholding and no manufacturing in China or with Chinese companies — on sourced evidence? Re-check the China screen for the top-tier targets. Are the top-tier targets really accessible to a financial sponsor in their home market? Would strategic buyers outbid a sponsor? Are exit routes supported by completed deals?
8. **Manufacturing-partner thesis.** Is Western demand for Asia ex-China manufacturing evidenced or assumed? Would the Western partner capture the economics, leaving the manufacturer with thin margins and concentration risk? What stops it switching supplier, building its own plant, or being required by policy to manufacture at home?
9. **New-build theses.** Is the market really proven, or only forecast? Is the solution's maturity overstated — what has been measured independently, at what scale, and by whom? Why would the technology owner manufacture through this new company and not in-house, at its existing foundry, or with an established contract manufacturer? Can a minority investor protect its position? What is lost if it fails?
10. **Geopolitics.** Export controls, foreign-investment review, IP and supply-chain exposure.
11. **Framing.** Was the programme anchored on AI data-centre optics by its starting catalog? Reopen rejected or adjacent opportunities if the evidence suggests they are better. Say plainly if the geography rule has pushed the programme into second-best assets, and what the best opportunities outside the rule would have been, so the user can see the cost of the constraint.
12. **For each surviving thesis:** the three strongest objections, the indicators that would disconfirm it, and its kill conditions. Separate risks that can be mitigated from those that cannot be controlled.

## Deliverables
A. **Red-team report:** findings by thesis, each rated fatal / serious / manageable, with evidence.

B. **Re-verification results** for the sampled claims and the recalculated figure.

C. **Revised ranking** of opportunities after challenge, including any reopened ideas. "None survive" is an acceptable result.

D. **Questions the user must answer** before a final recommendation.

E. **Gate self-assessment and Stage Handoff.**

## Gate pass criteria
- Ten claims re-verified in this session, with results reported.
- At least one return figure recalculated.
- Each surviving thesis has three evidence-based objections and explicit kill conditions.
- At least one rejected or adjacent opportunity has been reconsidered.
- Every finding is rated for severity.

**Stop after Stage 6A.** The user reviews the objections before any final recommendation is written.

# Stage 6B — Final investment committee package

**Required context:** everything provided for 6A, plus the approved 6A report and the user's responses to it. This may continue in the 6A session.

**Recommended model:** Sonnet 5.5 is sufficient; staying on Fable 5.2 in the same chat is also fine.

## Mandate
Deliver an IC-ready **PE investment focus strategy** that incorporates the red-team findings. A recommendation to defer, to focus on adjacent established industrial businesses, or to avoid the sector is acceptable. Do not force a positive decision, and do not present an optimistic technology report as an investment recommendation.

## The IC package covers
1. **Executive verdict:** invest, defer, investigate further or reject; recommended strategy; confidence.
2. **Industry case:** growth drivers, adoption paths, competition, value chain, margins and profit pools.
3. **Focus thesis:** exact segments and boundaries, business models, ideal targets, geographies, timing, and why PE can win.
4. **Investment selection:** scored alternatives, rejected segments and reasons.
5. **Transaction strategy:** platform archetype, control route, deal-size bands, acquisition universe and sourcing.
6. **Underwriting:** valuation evidence, key assumptions, return scenarios, break-even conditions, financing and thresholds.
7. **Value creation:** controllable actions, metrics, bolt-ons, capital allocation, 100-day / 2-year / 5-year priorities.
8. **Risk, counter-thesis and kill criteria:** the red-team objections and how each was resolved, accepted or left open.
9. **Exit strategy:** buyer categories, precedents, alternatives, scale requirements, weak-market downside.
10. **Next steps:** research, outreach, target screening and a 30/90/180-day roadmap.

Include an evidence-confidence table, source references by ID, a list of assumptions that need confidential diligence, and the **mandate-dependent conclusions**: what changes with fund size, return target, fund domicile and control preference if these were not provided. Restate the geography rule and China screen as applied, including the treatment of Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan, and of Chinese customers and suppliers.

## Required final outputs
A. **IC executive decision memo** (two pages at most).

B. **Full investment thesis and focus strategy** with segment and counter-thesis analysis.

C. **Prioritised target and origination programme.**

D. **Underwriting summary** with traceable assumptions, and the model file if tools allow.

E. **Red-team objections with their disposition, kill criteria and open diligence requests.**

F. **Execution roadmap and final evidence register.**

## Final pass criteria
- Every fatal or serious red-team finding is explicitly resolved, accepted as a risk, or left open with a named diligence step.
- The verdict states what to invest in **if anything**, where not to invest, why the selected businesses should capture sustainable cash flows, how PE creates value, what must be true for the case to work, what would invalidate it and how equity value is realised.
- Confidence is stated for each major conclusion, and nothing graded C or D is presented as fact.
